Questions agencies ask

The short answers. Anything else, just ask.

What do you deliver?

Instagram Reels, Stories and posts filmed on-ground at the event, with your brand placed naturally in the matchday content: on screen, in the caption, or as a “presented by” series.

How quickly is content published?

Matchday content goes live while the moment is still trending. Exact posting times are agreed with you before the event.

Can we reuse the content on our own channels?

Yes. Usage rights, duration and whether you can boost the posts are agreed as part of each deal.

What reporting do we get?

A campaign report from Instagram Insights covering views, reach, likes, saves, shares and audience response, prepared by a data analyst with 7+ years of experience.

Where do you cover events?

Across the UAE, mainly Dubai International Stadium, Sharjah Cricket Stadium and major venues such as Coca-Cola Arena.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is negotiable and depends on the event, the number of deliverables and usage rights. Message on WhatsApp for a quote.
